# Gozec Whitepaper

## Vision

People should be able to use open networks without turning their lives into a public feed. Gozec makes quiet the normal setting rather than a special mode for the few who know where to look.

## Mission

Build tools that let anyone act within Solana with composure: payments that do not broadcast intent, work that stays sealed until its owner decides otherwise, and proof that any stranger can check.
