    THE LONG VIEW

    Where Gozec is headed.

    Vision

    People should be able to use open networks without turning their lives into a public feed. Gozec exists to make quiet the normal setting, not a special mode for the few who know where to look.

    Build tools that let anyone act within Solana with composure: payments that do not broadcast intent, work that stays sealed until the owner decides otherwise, and proof that anyone can check.
